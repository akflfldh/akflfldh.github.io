COLOR MOVE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Color Move (Android package name: com.colormovepuzzle.game), provided by HJH82, handles information when you use the game.

1. Game data stored on your device

Color Move does not require an account to play. Game progress, unlocked stages, the current puzzle state, and settings are stored locally on your device. These gameplay records are used to save and restore your progress and preferences. As currently designed, the game does not transmit these gameplay records to a developer-operated server.

2. Advertising and third-party data processing

Color Move uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertisements. According to Google's SDK disclosures, this SDK may automatically collect and share the following data:

  • IP address, which can be used to estimate general location;
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views;
  • Diagnostic information, such as performance and stability information; and
  • Device and other identifiers, including advertising identifiers where available and permitted.

Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and related purposes, subject to applicable settings and user choices. Information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners in accordance with their policies.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.

3. Consent and privacy choices

Where required, Color Move uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present advertising consent and privacy choice messages. When applicable, you can review or change your choices using the privacy options provided in the game. You can also manage advertising identifiers and related preferences through your Android device settings.

4. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored progress and settings generally remain on your device until you clear the application's data or uninstall the game, subject to any device backup or restoration features. To delete local game data, use your device's app storage settings to clear Color Move's data. This will also remove saved game progress.

Data processed by Google or other advertising partners is subject to their respective privacy and retention policies. For questions or privacy requests related to Color Move, contact the developer using the address below.

5. Data security

According to Google's documentation, data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS). No method of data transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's privacy

Advertising and privacy settings are configured according to the game's declared target audience and applicable requirements. If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about information relating to a child, please contact us.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game's functionality, services, or data practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
